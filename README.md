An AI judge picks the winner, who takes the $2 reward.

## The entry

> I'm out of office until Monday — your email has been forwarded to my out-of-office reply, which is also out of office until Monday.

The sender isn't just away — they helpfully forwarded your email to their own auto-reply, which is also away until Monday. It's an infinite loop of nobody ever answering, dressed up as customer service. The joke is that the system meant to tell you when they'll be back has itself gone on holiday.

## What this does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic — no network, clock, or randomness) validates the entry (single line, 1–140 characters, no leading/trailing whitespace, no double spaces), prints it to stdout, and also writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT` when set, falling back to `./output/entry.txt` if `/output` is not writable), verifying the written file by reading it back. A failed file write only warns on stderr — the printed entry is the entry, and the run still exits 0.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Exits 0 on success. The entry text is printed to stdout and written to `entry.txt` in the output directory.
